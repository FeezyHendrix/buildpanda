# Commercial dogfooding findings — Ikorodu–Sagamu Link Road (Section 2)

Reviewer: QS / procurement lead ("QA Reviewer", workspace Precon QA Builders). Date: 13 Sep 2026.
Project: http://localhost:5173/project/prj_99ea3fe0-16a7-4c83-a333-dc3ba79611f1
Severity scale: bug / wrong-for-construction / confusing / missing / cosmetic.

## Suppliers

1. [missing] Suppliers page — Add supplier drawer has name / contact / email / phone / address / notes only. No trade / category / material supplied, no approved-supplier status, no lead time, no payment terms. A QS keeps an approved-supplier list by trade (aggregates, cement, steel, fuel…) and needs to filter by it when placing an order. I had to bury "Trade: laterite" in Notes. Console: none.
2. [wrong-for-construction] Suppliers are **project-level** (created 8 on the road project; the other project "2 bedroom Flat Interior" shows "No suppliers yet"). Dangote, Total Energies and Federated Steel are company-wide accounts a contractor buys from on every job; a QS expects a company supplier register with per-project usage, not re-typing the supplier on each project. Compounded by (3).
3. [bug] Suppliers — typed the same supplier two ways ("Ogun Quarries" then "Ogun Quarries Ltd", same email and phone). Accepted with no duplicate warning; two cards now sit side by side. Expected: at least a "looks like an existing supplier (same email/phone)" warning, ideally a case/suffix-insensitive uniqueness check per project. Console: none.
4. [confusing] Suppliers — after saving from the Add supplier drawer via the primary button, the `[role=dialog]` element stays mounted and reports visible with the previously saved values still filled in (detected via DOM after each of 7 sequential adds); the list updates behind it. Pressing Escape clears it. Low impact but it's why a second "Add supplier" click re-shows old values if the drawer never fully unmounts.
5. [cosmetic] Suppliers list is alphabetically sorted with no search or filter; fine for 9 rows, will not scale to a real supplier register (30–60 suppliers on a road job).

## Material orders (Materials & Equipment → Materials)

6. [wrong-for-construction] Materials → New material order / Edit material order — there is **no stage (phase) or activity picker** anywhere in the form, yet the drawer subtitle says "Connect the request to the work it unlocks" and every card renders "Phase: Unlinked · Activity: Unlinked". All 5 real orders (laterite, cement, rebar, diesel, precast culvert) are therefore unlinked; I had to write the stage/activity into Lifecycle notes. A QS needs the link to drive "material coverage" on the activity, to know which activity a late delivery delays, and for cost allocation to the stage. (Checked Edit too: same fields, no link.) Console: none.
7. [wrong-for-construction] Materials → New material order — **Supplier is a free-text box** ("Optional", no datalist, no autocomplete). The 9 suppliers on the Suppliers page cannot be picked, so there is no relation between an order and a supplier record: no supplier contact on the order, no per-supplier spend, and the "typed two ways" edge case is guaranteed (order 1 says "Ogun Quarries", the edge-case order says "Ogun Quarries Ltd" and nothing connects them). Expected: supplier picker fed by the supplier register, with "add new" inline.
8. [confusing] Materials → New material order — Quantity 0 silently **disables** the Create order button; no validation message, no red field, no tooltip. A user staring at a greyed button has no idea why. Expected: "Quantity must be greater than 0" under the field. (Quantity input has no `min` attribute either.)
9. [wrong-for-construction] Materials → New material order — needed-by date **in the past** (01 Sep 2026, 12 days ago) is accepted silently. The card renders "Needed 01 Sept 2026" like any other; no "overdue" / "late" chip, no confirmation. A request that is already late on creation is either a data-entry mistake or an emergency — both need surfacing.
10. [cosmetic] Materials card — date formatting is inconsistent: "Needed 01 Sept 2026" / "14 Sept 2026" (four-letter Sept) vs "12 Oct 2026" / "16 Nov 2026". Use a consistent 3-letter month.
11. [confusing] Materials → order card — "Doc: No receipt/spec" is shown on every card but the create/edit form has no document attach; unclear where a delivery note / spec would be attached from.
12. [missing] Materials → New material order — no unit price × quantity; only a lump "Estimated cost". A QS prices orders as rate × quantity (₦4,500/m³ × 3,000 m³); the lump sum cannot be reconciled to the BoQ rate or to a partial delivery (1,800 m³ delivered = ₦8.1M, not ₦13.5M).
13. [wrong-for-construction] Materials → status ladder. Every transition is a single "Move to X" button with **no dialog**: no approver, no approval date, no PO number on "Ordered", no delivered quantity / delivery date / delivery-note number / received-by on "Partially delivered" / "Delivered". Diesel went Requested → Approved → Ordered → Partially delivered → Delivered in four clicks with zero data captured. A delivery is a contractual event (GRN); a QS needs qty received vs ordered, date, DN number, who signed.
14. [wrong-for-construction] Materials → the ladder is strictly linear: an Ordered item's only forward button is "Move to **Partially** delivered" — a complete single-drop delivery (8,000 L diesel in one bowser) must be recorded as "partially delivered" first. There is no way to record the actual partial quantity either (laterite 1,800 of 3,000 m³ is just the label "Partially delivered"; the card still says 3000 m³ and ₦13.5M committed).
15. [missing] Materials → no **Cancel** / **Rejected** / **On hold** state and no backward transition. "Cancel an Ordered order" is only possible via Delete, which erases the record (the dialog even says "removes the material request from the lifecycle board"). A cancelled PO must remain on file with a reason; a rejected load (CBR fail) must be recorded against the supplier. I recorded the rejected load as a note + a separate re-order titled "Laterite — replacement for rejected load (CBR fail)". Delete uses an in-app dialog (good, not window.confirm).
16. [bug] Materials KPI "Open material orders" counts Delivered orders (showed 7 with 1 delivered, 6 after deleting an unrelated duplicate — still includes the Delivered diesel). "Open" should exclude Delivered (and cancelled).
17. [missing] Materials → no **expected delivery date** / supplier-confirmed date field, so a supplier saying "21 Nov instead of 16 Nov" cannot be captured as data — only free text in Lifecycle notes. Without it the app cannot compute "late" (expected > needed-by) or feed the activity's start.
18. [confusing] Materials → "Move to Approved" is exposed to whoever created the request, with no separation between requester (site agent) and approver (PM/QS). On a real job the site agent raises, the QS/PM approves against budget.
19. [bug] Materials — a batched sequence of creates that timed out in the browser left a **duplicate** "Precast box culvert units" order (two identical cards). The form does not de-duplicate identical title+material+needed-by submissions within seconds; the second submit should be idempotent or the button should disable while pending. Deleted the duplicate.
20. [confusing] Materials → "Import from BoQ" dialog — header text says "Upload a PDF, Excel or CSV bill of quantities" but the dropzone says "PDF, JPG or PNG (max. 10MB)". Excel/CSV vs JPG/PNG contradict each other; a QS's BoQ is almost always .xlsx.

## Material log

21. [wrong-for-construction] Material log — moving the diesel order to **Delivered did not create a ledger entry**; the Material log stayed at 0 entries until I logged the receipt again by hand. Two disconnected records of the same physical delivery (order status vs stock ledger) will drift; the GRN should be one event that both closes the order and books stock.
22. [missing] Material log → Log material — **no date field** (every entry is stamped "Just now"/TODAY), no supplier, no delivery-note number, no link to the material order, no unit rate. A receipt logged on Monday for Friday's delivery is wrong in the ledger, and an audit cannot tie the stock entry to the DN or PO. The material combobox does helpfully list the material names from open orders.
23. [confusing] Material log — the person who logs an entry can **approve their own entry** ("Approve this received so it counts toward stock" is offered to the logger). Maker/checker is the right idea, but self-approval defeats it; at minimum flag "approved by the same person who logged it".
24. [bug] Material log — after approving the "Used 120 bags" entry before the receipt was approved, stock correctly showed −120 and the entry got a red "Negative stock" chip. After the 800-bag receipt was approved, stock is 680 bags but the **"Negative stock" chip on the Used row persists** (stale flag). Screenshot: shots/commercial/material-log-stale-negative-flag.jpg
25. [confusing] Material log — issuing (Used) more than is in approved stock is allowed with no warning at log time; the negative only appears after approval. Expected: warn on the form "Only 0 bags on hand".
26. [cosmetic] Material log — stock figures are right after approval (800 in − 120 out = 680 bags; 8,000 L diesel). "Needs attention" correctly drops to 0. Ledger shows "Void" per row (good: append-only).

## Material approvals

27. [missing] Material approvals → Request approval — **no file attachment**. A material approval IS the sample/certificate/datasheet (laterite lab report, quarry grading certificate, mill certs); the form only has free-text Specification/Notes. I had to quote certificate numbers in text. Expected: attach one or more documents (or pick from Documents) and show them on the card for the approver.
28. [bug / audit] Material approvals — a **Rejected** request can be **edited** (Edit → Save changes) and the specification text is silently replaced while the status stays Rejected and the rejection comment still refers to the old sample. Decided records must be read-only (or edits versioned). I reverted my edit and raised a new resubmission request instead. Same for Approved: card still offers Edit/Delete after approval.
29. [confusing] Material approvals — Reject says "The requester will need to raise a new request" but there is no "Resubmit as new" / "Duplicate" action on the rejected card; the resubmission has to be retyped from scratch. "Request changes" → "Resubmit" state works, but after the requester edits, the card stays in "Resubmit" (no "Resubmitted / back to Pending" transition) — the approver cannot tell whether the requester has actually acted.
30. [wrong-for-construction] Material approvals — the approval is not linked to the material order or supplier record. Laterite order to Ogun Quarries was already **Ordered** (13 Sep) when the source sample was rejected; the app raised no flag on either side. Approving after the order was placed is accepted silently (I noted it in the request). Expected: an order whose material has a Rejected/Pending approval should show "Awaiting material approval" and warn on "Move to Ordered".
31. [confusing] Material approvals — the reviewer dropdown lists only project members ("QA Reviewer" = me), and I can approve/reject my own request. On a public-works job the approver is the client's resident engineer (Engr. Bolanle Adeyemi) who must be on the team as a client-side participant with a "material approver" role; nothing indicates who is allowed to approve.
32. [cosmetic] Material approvals — unit list is a fixed set (ea/m/m2/m3/kg/tonne/bag/roll/litre/set) with ASCII "m3", while Materials uses a free combobox with "m³". Inconsistent units across two pages for the same material.
33. [cosmetic] Material approvals works well otherwise: decision dialogs capture a response, decisions are attributed with name + time, and the cards group into "Resubmit requested / Awaiting decision / Decided" sensibly. Screenshot: shots/commercial/material-approvals.jpg

## Equipment requests

34. [wrong-for-construction] Equipment requests — same pattern as materials: the form says "Tie equipment rentals to schedule dates, site activities, supplier paperwork" but has **no phase/activity picker** and Supplier is free text; every card reads "Phase: Unlinked · Activity: Unlinked · Doc: No supplier doc". Plant hire is the second-largest cost on a road job and is booked per activity (grader → cut to formation); without the link the app cannot show plant coverage per activity or hire cost per stage.
35. [wrong-for-construction] Equipment requests — Requested → Approved → Scheduled → On hire → Returned are one-click "Move to …" with **no dialog**: no on-hire date, no off-hire/return date, no plant/fleet number, no condition on return, no hire-rate. I moved the grader to "Returned" on 13 Sep for a hire that starts 05 Oct — accepted without a word. "Return the grader early" can only be expressed by editing "Needed until" (05 Oct → 20 Oct) and writing a note; the ₦9.6M estimate does not re-compute for the shorter hire (no rate × duration).
36. [missing] Equipment requests — "Extend the roller hire" = edit Needed until (18 Dec → 15 Jan) and retype the cost by hand. No extension record, no history of the original off-hire date, no variation to the hire order. A QS needs the original vs extended period for the plant-hire reconciliation.
37. [wrong-for-construction] Equipment requests — needed-from date in the past (01 Sep) accepted silently; no "overdue"/"late" marker on the Requests tab.
38. [confusing] Equipment requests — the "Schedule" tab is just the list of Approved cards ("Approved to book"), not a calendar/Gantt of hire periods; the tab name implies a plant schedule. "Booked cost" on each tab only sums the cards in that tab (Schedule ₦11.2M, Returns ₦9.6M) — there is no total committed hire spend anywhere.
39. [confusing] Equipment requests — the Returns tab subtitle says "Closed or cancelled" but there is no Cancel action (only Delete), same gap as materials. Cards stay editable after Returned.
40. [cosmetic] Equipment requests — the tab you were on is lost when a card moves to the next bucket (card silently disappears from the current tab; you must know to click the next tab). A toast "Moved to On hire — view" would help. Screenshot: shots/commercial/equipment-schedule-tab.jpg

## Purchase orders (Finance → Expenses → Purchase orders)

41. [wrong-for-construction] Purchase orders are **not connected to material requests** or to the supplier register: "raise a PO from the cement request" is impossible — no "Create PO" on the material card, no request picker on the PO form, Vendor is free text (retyped "Dangote Ikorodu depot"). The two records (material order status "Approved", PO "Received") now disagree and nothing reconciles them. Only the PO form has a Stage picker; materials/equipment do not.
42. [bug] Purchase orders — a **Draft** PO is counted in "Committed spend" (₦6.8M shown immediately on creation as Draft) although the form itself says "Issued POs count as committed cost for this stage". Same for **Cancelled**: after cancelling PO-IS2-002 (₦24M) "Committed spend" stayed ₦30.8M. Committed should be Issued + PartiallyReceived + Received (+Closed), never Draft or Cancelled.
43. [wrong-for-construction] Purchase orders — status is a free **dropdown on the Edit form** (Draft/Issued/PartiallyReceived/Received/Closed/Cancelled), even at creation (a PO can be born "Received" or "Cancelled"). No Issue / Receive / Cancel actions with dates, no issued-by, no received quantity per line. "Receive more than ordered" cannot be expressed as a receipt: the only way is to **edit the line quantity on an already-Received PO** (800 → 850 accepted, committed changed to ₦7,225,000, no warning, no audit) — i.e. the PO value can be rewritten after receipt. Reverted to 800.
44. [bug] Purchase orders — Cancelled → Received and Cancelled → Issued are correctly refused by the backend, but the UI shows the raw **"Request failed with status code 409"** in the drawer, no explanation, and the status select still offers the forbidden options. A cancelled PO cannot be un-cancelled even when cancelled by mistake (I had to raise PO-IS2-003 to re-issue the rebar). Screenshot: shots/commercial/po-409-raw-error.jpg
45. [cosmetic] Purchase orders — PO number is typed by hand ("e.g. PO-0042"), no sequence, no uniqueness check; "PartiallyReceived" is shown unspaced in the select; dates render as ISO "Ordered 2026-09-13" while the rest of the app uses "13 Sep 2026".
46. [bug] Purchase orders vs Finance overview — "Committed spend" on the PO tab (₦54.8M, includes the Cancelled PO-IS2-002) disagrees with Finance → Overview "Committed — Issued purchase orders" (₦30.8M). Two committed figures for the same project on adjacent pages.
47. [wrong-for-construction] Finance → Expenses — neither the **Delivered** diesel order (₦8M) nor the **Received** cement PO (₦6.8M) produced an expense / cost against the stage ("Total spend ₦0.00", stage "Total Costs ₦0.00" on Finance overview). The Materials delete dialog even promises "Delivered finance receipts remain in finance history" — there is no such receipt. Delivered material and received POs are actual cost and must hit the stage cost-vs-budget.

## Delay scenario 3 — late & short laterite delivery (supplier delay)

48. [wrong-for-construction] There is **no data path from a material order to the programme**. I recorded "arrives 21 Nov, 1,800 of 3,000 m³" the only way the app allows (Lifecycle notes + status "Partially delivered"). Nothing reacted: activity "Import laterite & spread" stayed Planned 16 Nov – 08 Dec 0 %, the Overview schedule KPI and needs-attention list ignore it, the look-ahead "Coming up" cards still say "No materials ordered" for every activity (this per-activity material coverage exists on the Look aheads page but can never be populated because orders have no activity link). The only way to make the programme react is to separately "Log a delay" on the activity by hand.
49. [wrong-for-construction] Site activity → "Log a delay" — the reason list (Client Approval, Design Change, Inspection Hold, Permit Delay, Equipment Breakdown, Equipment Unavailable, Crew Shortage, Subcontractor Delay, Delivery Delay, Material Shortage, Rain, Other…) has **no culpability / EOT flag** and no link to the order or supplier. Nothing stops a supplier delay being logged as "Client Approval" and later claimed as client delay — the app does not distinguish excusable (EOT-able) from culpable delay at all. The form also has no "days lost" (only a start date-time and a cost), so "5 days late" cannot be entered. Row 20 already shows "₦2,400,000 · 1 open" delay from another workstream; there is no way to open that delay from the activity row (row menu = Edit/Delete only; "1 open" is not clickable).
50. [missing] Site activity table has no activity detail view (drawer/page) — no place to see linked orders, plant, approvals or delays for an activity; "material coverage" is not visible from the activity, only as "No materials ordered" on Look aheads.

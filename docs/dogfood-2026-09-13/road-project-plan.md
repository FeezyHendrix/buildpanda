# Ikorodu–Sagamu Link Road Rehabilitation (Section 2) — project plan

Client: Lagos State Ministry of Works (public works client). Contractor: Precon QA Builders (our workspace).
Scope: rehabilitate 2.4 km of a two-lane single carriageway (7.3 m carriageway + 1.5 m shoulders), including
three box culverts, 1.9 km of lined side drains, full pavement reconstruction (sub-base, crushed-stone base,
60 mm asphalt binder, 40 mm wearing course), kerbs, signage and thermoplastic line marking.
Site address: Km 14 Ikorodu–Sagamu Road, Odogunyan, Ikorodu, Lagos. Currency NGN.

Contract: NGN 850,000,000 (re-measurement contract, monthly interim valuations, 5 % retention to
practical completion, 10 % mobilisation advance recovered pro-rata from IPC 2 onward, 12-month
defects liability). Commencement 7 Sept 2026; completion 26 Mar 2027 (29 weeks). Liquidated
damages NGN 850,000/day capped at 10 %.

## Delay scenarios (the heart of managing a road job — enact every one and check the cascade)
For each delay: raise it on the activity with the correct reason category, note the days, then check (a) the activity's dates and status, (b) successor activities on the programme / Gantt, (c) the look-ahead that covered it, (d) the key date it feeds, (e) the overview's schedule KPI and needs-attention list, (f) the daily log for that day, (g) Panda AI's answer to "what is delaying the project".
1. Weather — 10 Sep heavy rain, site flooded at ch 1+150: "Plant mobilisation" and "Hoarding & site security" lose 1 day each. Reason: weather. Non-culpable (EOT-able, no cost).
2. Utility strike — 14 Oct excavation for culvert 1 hits an unmarked NNPC pipeline at ch 0+425; NNPC stop-work for 6 working days; "Excavate & blind culvert 1" and "Cast culvert 1 base & walls" slip 6 days; RFI 1 raised, change request 2 (relocate culvert) follows; key date "Culverts complete" should move to 05 Dec. Reason: third party / unforeseen conditions. Claim EOT 6 days + cost.
3. Late material — laterite first delivery (3,000 m³ needed 16 Nov) arrives 21 Nov and 1,800 m³ only; "Import laterite & spread" starts 5 days late; sub-base key date at risk. Reason: supplier. Culpable to contractor (no EOT) — the app should NOT let this be claimed as client delay without a flag.
4. Late client approval — TMP approval expected 18 Sep, Ministry approves 25 Sep; "TMP approval & signage" slips 7 days, "Break out failed asphalt" cannot start under single-lane working until then. Reason: client / approvals. Key date "TMP approved" missed. EOT 7 days.
5. Plant breakdown — 9 Dec the only asphalt-plant-to-site paver is unavailable? (too early) — instead: 8 Oct the motor grader breaks down for 3 days during "Cut to formation"; reason: plant. Culpable.
6. Late payment — mobilisation advance invoice sent 8 Sep, due 22 Sep, paid 3 Oct: overdue for 11 days; note whether the finance pages, overview and notifications show it as overdue and whether cash-flow reflects the late receipt.
7. Late RFI response — RFI 2 (CBR requirement) due 20 Sep, resident engineer answers 29 Sep; "Compact & CBR tests" cannot be planned; RFI overdue state, ball-in-court on the client.
8. Community disruption — 30 Sep road users block the tip route for a day; "Cart away to approved tip" loses 1 day; reason: other / third party.
Then: raise an extension-of-time claim summarising the EOT-able delays (weather 1 + utility 6 + client approval 7 = 14 days) — does the app have anywhere to record an EOT and move practical completion from 26 Mar to 09 Apr 2027? If not, record it as a change request "Extension of time — 14 days, no cost" and note the gap. Check liquidated damages exposure is not shown as due while an EOT is pending.

One week running a road job in BuildPanda

A 2.4 km road rehabilitation — 12 priced stages, 32 activities, ₦850m re-measurement contract — set up and managed through the product exactly as a PM, site agent, QS and admin would, with the awkward days deliberately included: rain, a pipeline strike, short deliveries, a late client approval, a late payment, an extension-of-time claim.

Fix these first

twelve items, root cause located where known
  1. Weather and inspection-category chips submit the form
    The chips in the daily-log conditions dialog and the inspection request are buttons without type="button", so clicking one submits with the stale value. Weather can never be saved from the UI; every inspection is "Structural". upsert-daily-log-dialog.tsx, inspection request form.
  2. A delay is a note, not a schedule event
    No days lost, no end, no culpability or extension-of-time flag, no link to an RFI, order or change; it cannot be opened or resolved once logged. Nine open delays leave the Gantt at "Timeline shift 0 d", key dates untouched, the look-ahead unaware, and the Gantt draws each open delay from its start to the activity's planned end, so a one-day rain stop renders as six days.
  3. Deleting a stage with activities returns 500 and the UI hides it
    The composite foreign key (phase_id, building_id) ON DELETE SET NULL tries to null activities.building_id, which is NOT NULL. The dialog closes, the stage stays, nothing is said. Migration 20260790_programme_building_scope.ts.
  4. "Optional" location makes every new activity fail with 400
    The form sends an empty string; the schema says minLength: 1. The only feedback is "Request failed with status code 400" under the Panda AI bubble. activities/routes.ts line 60.
  5. Payments have no guards
    ₦90m accepted against an ₦85m balance; a second payment on a paid invoice leaves balance −₦1m; a Draft invoice can be paid straight to Paid; future-dated receipts accepted; a paid invoice can be hard-deleted with its receipts.
  6. Rich-text editors carry the previous record into the next one
    Raise RFI (RFI 3 saved with RFI 2's question, and there is no Edit RFI), New task (task 3 shipped with task 2's description), and the change-request edit dialog loses the saved reason.
  7. Server errors are swallowed on the finance and setup pages
    Contract document upload 500, invoice save 400 (Trade is required but hidden under "More options"), "Mark executed" 400, stage delete 500 — all silent. Elsewhere the raw Axios string is shown instead of the server's message (duplicate invite 409, cancelled PO 409).
  8. The Client role preset says finance is hidden but grants it
    The card reads "Finance details hidden by default"; the matrix ticks FINANCES View and Dispute; the backend enforces one flat finances.view. A Ministry resident engineer invited with the preset can read contractor costs, expenses and margin via the URL or API. Needs a client-facing versus internal split.
  9. Four pages give four answers to "how much have we spent and been paid"
    Project overview: Budget used ₦0, Cost variance ₦0, 0 change orders, Cash paid ₦85m. Finance overview waterfall: Certified ₦0, Paid ₦0 (it is fed by funding deposits, not by invoice payments). Payments tab: paid ₦85m. Phases: ₦20.75m used on stage 1. Invoices have no contract link, so certified-to-date can never see them.
  10. Progress cannot be set anywhere
    Not in the activity drawer, the table or the Gantt; PATCH /activities/:id { percentComplete } returns 200 and stores nothing. "Construction progress 0%" for the life of the job.
  11. Panda AI does not know today's date
    It reported orders needed in October as "past their needed-by dates", missed the one order that is late, treated a future-dated log as a normal day, and drafted a client report that opens "Overall status: On track, amount spent 0 NGN" against an overview that says behind plan with ₦6.4m in the ledger. The system prompt carries no current date.
  12. Orders are not linked to anything
    Material and equipment forms have no stage or activity picker although every card prints "Phase: Unlinked · Activity: Unlinked"; supplier is free text so the supplier register is decorative; delivered goods and received POs never reach expenses or stage cost; "Committed" counts Draft and Cancelled POs.

Extension of time

  • No EOT register, revised completion date or liquidated damages anywhere (zero hits in frontend or backend).
  • Recorded as change request "CR-003 Extension of time — 14 days, no cost"; even approved, +14 days moves nothing.
  • Practical completion stays 26 Mar 2027; LD exposure is never shown, so the "not due while an EOT is pending" check cannot be made.

Culpability

  • The reason list has no client / contractor / neutral attribution and no utility-strike, third-party or late-RFI reasons.
  • A supplier delay can be logged as "Client approval" and nothing challenges it.
  • Panda AI's "EOT: yes/no" values were parsed from the free-text description, not from data.

What Panda AI said

  • Q · what is outstanding on the contract — accurate contract fields; never mentioned the ₦85m advance, the sent IPC, retention or the remaining value. "All phases are pending" while two are in progress.
  • Q · which orders are late — "These materials are past their needed-by dates" for three orders needed in October; the one September order that is late was omitted.
  • Q · summarise this week's diary — headcounts and weather right; a log dated 15 Sep treated as a normal day; no activities mentioned.
  • Q · what changed on culvert 1 — "I could not find any information" despite two delayed activities, a High risk and a task; it only checked drawing markups.
  • Q · what is delaying the project — all nine delays and ₦5.32m correct; October delays presented as current; no EOT split; no effect on completion.
  • Weekly client report — well structured; opens "Overall Status: On Track" and "Amount Spent: 0 NGN".

Why it goes wrong

  • The agent's system prompt carries no current date, so "late", "this week" and "future" are guesses.
  • Tools return records, not derived positions: nothing computes outstanding, certified, late or slipped, so the model reads a phase dump and stops.
  • It picks one tool per question (markups for "culvert 1") and does not widen the search on an empty result.
  • Delay days and EOT are inferred from free text because the record has no such fields.

Does the overview tell the truth?

Partly. It is honest about what was typed and misleading about what it means. Schedule says "Behind plan · 9 delayed activities" when seven of those delays are dated October and the honest line on 13 Sep is one day lost to rain and a six-day slip booked at culvert 1. Budget used and cost variance read ₦0 against ₦20.75m of expenses, ₦30.8m committed and ₦4.2m of approved variation. The completion date, extension-of-time position and liquidated-damages exposure — the three numbers a road PM is actually managing — appear nowhere on the page. Field activity counts pre-possession days and Sundays as missed. The Needs-attention list has the right idea and gets the one real item of the week (TMP approval due in five days, gating single-lane working) as a small chip.

What could not be tested here

File store offline

  • The object store on port 9000 (MinIO) is not running and Docker cannot be started beside the native stack, so every POST /files returned 500.
  • Untested: plan upload, review markups, revision supersession, duplicate files, document upload and categories, permit attachments, signed-contract and CO signing, photo attachments on logs and updates, BoQ import, programme import.
  • Product finding regardless: a storage outage surfaces as "Internal server error" or as nothing at all, and in two places the user's typed text was wiped when the upload failed.

Done through the API because the UI has no path

  • Weather on six days; 13 activity-hour links; voiding a future-dated day; reopening an RFI; correcting an RFI question; creating risks; funding the project with the advance; moving tasks between columns.
  • Each is recorded in the findings as a missing control, with the route that exists.

# BuildPanda — Announcement & Walkthrough Video Script

**Version:** 2026-06 (post-platform-update)
**Tone:** Confident product launch · for construction professionals, builders, and diaspora/Nigerian homeowners
**Runtime:** ~2:10 (announcement hook ~0:20 + walkthrough ~1:50)
**Format:** Spoken VO + on-screen action cues. Lines are timestamped scene beats.
**Brand:** BuildPanda blue `#004DE7`, clean white UI, the panda/logo motif.

> Notation — **VO:** narrator voiceover · **[SCREEN]** what's shown · **[TEXT]** on-screen caption/lower-third · **[SFX/MUSIC]** audio cue.

---

## 0:00 — COLD OPEN / ANNOUNCEMENT HOOK (≈20s)

**[SCREEN]** Black. The BuildPanda logo draws on in brand blue, then the wordmark resolves.
**[MUSIC]** Warm, building, confident — a single rising synth + soft percussion.

**VO:** "Building in Nigeria — or building from abroad — shouldn't mean flying blind."

**[SCREEN]** Quick montage, 4 frames in ~3s: a Lagos/Abuja construction site → a Gantt chart filling in → a 3D BIM model rotating → a chat thread lighting up with a notification.

**VO:** "So we rebuilt BuildPanda from the ground up. One platform. Every part of your build. In real time."

**[TEXT]** *BuildPanda — The construction command center.*
**[SCREEN]** Hard cut to the live dashboard: project cards, "Good morning" greeting, a "What's Next" panel.

---

## 0:20 — THE WORKSPACE (≈15s)

**[SCREEN]** The project workspace loads. Camera glides down the left sidebar; each nav item highlights as it's named.

**VO:** "From one workspace, you run the whole project — schedule, site activity, materials, finance, documents, and your team."

**[TEXT]** *Overview · Schedules · Site Control · Materials · Finance · Documents · Team*
**[SCREEN]** Cursor lands on **Overview** — health score, progress ring, budget, risk at a glance.

**VO:** "Open Overview and you see the health of your build in one glance — progress, budget, and risk, always current."

---

## 0:35 — SCHEDULE & PROGRESS (≈20s)

**[SCREEN]** Click **Schedules → Project Chart**. The editable Gantt appears, bars laid out across weeks.

**VO:** "Your programme of work lives in an editable Gantt. Drag a task to reschedule it —"

**[SCREEN]** Drag a bar to a new date; dependent bars and the phase range shift to follow. Undo/redo controls flash.

**VO:** "— and everything downstream adjusts, with full undo. Importing a programme? Drop the file in and BuildPanda parses your phases, activities, and key dates straight into the project."

**[SCREEN]** The "Import programme" dialog → a parsed preview of phases/activities → "Import into project."

**VO:** "And progress isn't busywork. Mark a site activity done —"

**[SCREEN]** On **Site Activity**, toggle an activity to *Completed*; the project progress ring ticks up in real time.

**[TEXT]** *Progress updates automatically as work gets done.*

---

## 0:55 — ASSIGN ANYONE TO ANYTHING + KANBAN (≈20s)

**[SCREEN]** A kanban board (Action Items / Queries / Change Requests) with avatars on cards.

**VO:** "Work has owners now. Every item — queries, RFIs, site activities, change requests, even BIM issues — can be assigned to a person."

**[SCREEN]** On a kanban card, open the inline **Assign** menu, pick a teammate; their avatar appears on the card. A bell notification pops in the top bar.

**VO:** "Assign straight from the card. They get notified instantly. That's why we built the board — so nothing falls through."

**[TEXT]** *Assign · Notify · Track — across every item type.*

---

## 1:15 — RFIs & BIM (3D) (≈22s)

**[SCREEN]** Open **RFIs**. An RFI detail drawer slides in from the right (~60% width) with a rich-text response editor.

**VO:** "Raise an RFI, route it to the right person, and respond with a proper editor — attach images, reference any activity or document, even reply by email."

**[SCREEN]** In the response, attach an image + insert a reference chip to an activity. Click "promote to change."

**VO:** "Coordinating in 3D? Upload your IFC model and open it right in the browser."

**[SCREEN]** Open **BIM Models** → the 3D viewer renders a building model; orbit it; click an element to drop a coordination issue pin.

**VO:** "Click any element to pin a coordination issue — and turn it into an RFI without leaving the model."

**[TEXT]** *RFIs + BIM — clash to resolution, in one place.*

---

## 1:37 — MESSAGING / CHAT (THE NEW FLAGSHIP) (≈25s)

**[SCREEN]** Click **Messages**. The full-screen chat opens: channel list, `# general`, a clean composer.

**VO:** "And now — BuildPanda has messaging. Channels and direct messages for everyone on the build."

**[SCREEN]** Type a message; trigger an `@`-mention dropdown; pick a teammate. Then click "+ Reference," search an RFI, attach it.

**VO:** "Mention anyone like you'd expect. Reference *anything* in the system — an RFI, an activity, a document — right inside the conversation."

**[SCREEN]** Send. The message appears instantly on a second device/window beside it (split screen) — **real-time**. Add a 👍 reaction; both update live.

**VO:** "It's real-time. React, reply in threads, pin what matters, and attach files and photos from the field."

**[SCREEN]** Hover a message → click **→ Task**. A toast: "Action item created from message." Cut to the Action Items board showing the new card.

**VO:** "See something that needs doing? Turn any message into an action item in one click. The conversation becomes the work."

**[TEXT]** *Messages — mention anyone, reference everything, in real time.*

---

## 2:02 — OWNER / CLIENT PORTAL (≈12s)

**[SCREEN]** Switch to the client portal view (`/my-build`) — a calmer, curated layout: Overview, Build Stages, RFIs, BIM, What's Next.

**VO:** "Invite your client or homeowner with one link. They get their own portal — scoped to just their project — so they can follow the build from anywhere in the world."

**[TEXT]** *Per-project access. Zero leaks. Total transparency.*

---

## 2:14 — CLOSE / CTA (≈10s)

**[SCREEN]** Pull back to the global dashboard: project cards + the **What's Next** panel aggregating what's due across every project.

**VO:** "Your whole portfolio. What's next, everywhere. One command center for the people who build."

**[SCREEN]** Logo lockup on white, blue underline draws in.
**[TEXT]** *BuildPanda — Build with confidence.*
**[TEXT]** *buildpanda.io*
**[MUSIC]** Resolves on a clean, confident note.

---

## Production notes

- **Capture real UI** at 3840×2160 (4K) where possible; the BuildPanda app is full-screen and brand-consistent.
- **Pacing:** ~3-5s per UI beat; never linger. Use subtle zoom/pan (Ken Burns) on static screens.
- **Real-time proof:** the split-screen "send on left, appears on right" shot is the money beat for Messaging — do not cut it.
- **Captions:** burn in the **[TEXT]** lower-thirds; keep them short and high-contrast (blue on white or white on blue).
- **VO:** warm, measured, confident — not hype. ~150 wpm.
- **Music:** one continuous bed, rising under the Messaging section, resolving at the logo.
